# Personal Portfolio

This context defines the words used to describe the portfolio’s work and the owner’s part in it.

## Work and recognition

**Portfolio Item**:
A project, team achievement, award, or significant team activity presented in this portfolio.
_Avoid_: Activity used as a synonym for Award

**Project**:
A piece of work built or implemented by the owner, alone or with a team. Its description records the owner’s own contribution separately from the overall project.
_Avoid_: Team project as if every part were done by the owner

**Team Award**:
A competition or hackathon result received by the team. The award describes the team’s placement or prize; it does not imply an individual award.
_Avoid_: Personal award, individual win

**Team Achievement**:
A meaningful result or milestone reached by a team, including a competition award or representing Thailand internationally. It describes a team outcome and does not imply that the portfolio owner completed every part of the work.
_Avoid_: Award used for every achievement

**Team Activity**:
A significant collaborative event or community contribution involving the portfolio owner. An activity can be included without a competition placement or prize.
_Avoid_: Project, award when no prize was received

**Team Story**:
A presentation of one team's competition season or significant activity, bringing its milestones and supporting evidence together. For Robocon, one story covers one year and identifies the outcome of each competition within it.
_Avoid_: One award assumed to describe every event in a season

**International Representation**:
Participation in an international competition as Thailand's representative. Any placement or prize is a separate team result.
_Avoid_: International championship title without a confirmed result

**Special Recognition**:
The highlighted Outstanding Student Award: “Diamond KMUTNB”, displayed before Projects. It is presented separately from competition and hackathon team results.

**Personal Contribution**:
The specific work the portfolio owner completed within a project, team achievement, or team activity.
_Avoid_: Team contribution without saying what the owner did

**Evidence**:
A supporting image, demonstration, repository, or other link associated with a portfolio item. Evidence is included only when available and relevant.
_Avoid_: Unsupported claim

## Visitor and profile

**Public Profile**:
The owner’s intentionally shared name, education, introduction, skills, and contact links.
_Avoid_: Private personal information

**Portfolio Visitor**:
A person who reads the deployed portfolio without signing in, including the course instructor.
_Avoid_: Admin, authenticated user

## Portfolio presentation

**Hero Visualization**:
The introductory illustration connecting the portfolio owner's work in AI, robotics, and software. It expresses the portfolio's identity and is separate from evidence of a specific project.
_Avoid_: Project evidence, live project demonstration
